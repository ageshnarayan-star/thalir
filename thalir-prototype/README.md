# THALIR
### *Empowering Every Child’s Journey*

A seed-funding evaluation clickable prototype for **THALIR**, a social-impact healthcare & edtech startup uniting special children, parents, educators, and multidisciplinary therapy teams.

---

## 🌟 Executive Summary & Problem Positioning

### The Problem
Children with neurodevelopmental delays, sensory processing sensitivities, and communication needs have deeply fragmented care:
- **School records** remain isolated in paper folders or teacher diaries.
- **Therapy notes** (speech, occupational, physical) remain locked in clinical software.
- **Home routines** and parent observations are rarely communicated effectively.
- Communication hurdles prevent children from expressing basic needs without stress.

### The Solution: THALIR
THALIR creates **one connected care ecosystem**:
1. **Parent-Controlled Care Passport:** A central, living child record with granular role-based access control.
2. **Progress Passport:** Continuous, collaborative tracking showing input from parents, teachers, and clinicians rather than siloed reports.
3. **Assistive AAC Communication:** Accessible, immediate visual communication boards with speech synthesis audio.
4. **School Connect:** Direct classroom observation feeds and synchronized IEP calendars.
5. **Verified Professional Support:** Trusted discovery for licensed developmental specialists.
6. **Ethical Early Awareness:** Educational guidance with strict non-diagnostic positioning and self-reflection tooling.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy & Seed-Pitch Aesthetic
- **Tone:** Trustworthy, calm, human, modern, inclusive, and technology-enabled.
- **Color Palette:** Warm organic canvas (`#F8FAF8`), deep forest green (`#1A3E33`), clinical sage accents (`#2D6A4F`, `#EBF5EE`), and crisp white card elevation.
- **Zero Gimmicks:** Strictly avoids cartoon characters, childish graphics, rainbow gradients, or toy-like buttons.
- **Brand Identity:** Pure typographic wordmark **THALIR** and exact tagline *"Empowering Every Child’s Journey"*. (No invented logo).
- **Dual Presentation Mode:**
  - **Phone Frame Mode:** iPhone 16 Pro mockup container with dynamic island and status bar (ideal for pitch decks, screencasts, and mobile walkthroughs).
  - **Expanded View:** Responsive web view for laptops, tablets, and projector screens.

## 📱 Interactive 15-Screen Architecture

1. **Screen 1 — Welcome:** Value proposition, exact brand mark & tagline, "Get Started" onboarding.
2. **Screen 2 — Choose Your Role:** Parent / Caregiver, Educator / School, Therapist / Professional, with privacy guarantee note.
3. **Screen 3 — Parent Dashboard:** Today's care overview, child selector, quantitative metrics (72%, 84%, 58%), 6 Quick Action tiles.
4. **Screen 4 — Child Profile:** Arjun (8 years), support domains, communication preferences, sensory accommodations, emergency contacts.
5. **Screen 5 — Progress Passport:** Domain cards with goals, latest observations, author badges, and interactive update creation.
6. **Sub-View — Progress History Timeline:** Chronological updates from Parent, School, and Therapist with stakeholder filters.
7. **Screen 6 — Care Passport:** The central child record with medical, school accommodations, and granular permissions sharing modal.
8. **Screen 7 — School Connect:** Classroom participation feed from Ms. Kavya, IEP calendar events, and "Message Teacher" modal.
9. **Screen 8 — Care Schedule:** Speech therapy, occupational therapy, and parent-teacher conference schedules with session prep.
10. **Screen 9 — Find Professionals:** Directory with filter pills (Speech, OT, Psychology, Behavioural, Special Ed, Physio), interactive Map View, and verified profile dialogs.
11. **Screen 10 — Assistive Communication:** 10 universally accessible AAC buttons (WATER, FOOD, HELP, TOILET, BREAK, YES, NO, HAPPY, SAD, UNCOMFORTABLE) with **Web Speech API voice playback**.
12. **Screen 11 — Early Awareness:** 6 developmental areas with clinical non-diagnostic disclaimer and 4-question self-reflection tool.
13. **Screen 12 — Family Resources:** Curated practical guides for Parents, Caregivers, and Educators with category search.
14. **Screen 13 — Notifications:** Multi-party notification feed with mark-as-read and direct screen navigation.
15. **Screen 14 — My Care Team:** Coordinated care circle (Ananya, Ms. Kavya, Dr. Meera, David Chen) with access levels and invite tool.
16. **Screen 15 — Settings & Privacy:** Data governance, parental control statement, profile management, and privacy logs.

## 🚀 How to Run the Prototype

### Option 1: Direct File Launch
Double click `index.html` in your file explorer or open with any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).

### Option 2: Local Python Server
Run the included lightweight local server:
```bash
python server.py
```
Open your browser at `http://localhost:8080/index.html`.
